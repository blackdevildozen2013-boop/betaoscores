01. Overview

Betão Scores is a sports information experience developed by OLEKSANDR PLACHYNDA (referred to in this policy as “the developer,” “we,” or “us”). This policy concerns the Betão Scores app distributed through the Apple App Store and the website at betaoscores.com. It explains the categories of information that can be involved in accessing sports content and communicating with the developer, together with relevant choices and safeguards.

Sports scores and fixtures shown on this website are provided through an external live-score service. Information on the website is not a guarantee of score accuracy or availability.

02. Information involved in the service

When you request sports information, network requests necessarily involve technical connection information such as an IP address, request time, and information needed to deliver the requested content. Depending on your device and the way content is delivered, service providers may also receive browser or device details. These details may be handled by the hosting provider or the sports data provider.

If you choose to contact us, the information you include in your message may contain your name, email address, and the content of your inquiry. When you submit the website contact form, your name, email address, selected topic and message are sent to Formspree, a third-party form delivery provider, to forward your inquiry to the developer. Formspree may process technical data necessary to operate and secure the service. Review Formspree’s privacy notice for more details.

The specific data categories collected by an installed version of the Betão Scores app depend on the app features and third-party services actually implemented. This policy does not represent that accounts, precise location, payments, advertising identifiers, or analytics SDKs are present in the app.

03. Why information is used

Technical connection information is used as needed to deliver requested pages and sports content, maintain service availability, troubleshoot errors, and protect services against abuse. Information voluntarily sent in a support or privacy inquiry is used to understand and respond to that inquiry and to handle relevant requests.

Where applicable law requires a legal basis, the basis may include providing the service you request, legitimate interests in maintaining secure and reliable services, compliance with legal obligations, or consent where required.

04. External sports data and other providers

The live-score panels on betaoscores.com embed scoreboards provided by Realtime Sports API. When the scoreboard loads, your browser connects directly to that provider, which may receive technical request information and process it under its own privacy terms. The scoreboard can display live scores, upcoming fixtures, and final results.

Website hosting and other infrastructure providers may process technical data needed to serve the website. We do not describe any additional app analytics, advertising, or tracking provider as active without verified implementation information. Data may be disclosed when required by law or necessary to protect legal rights, subject to applicable requirements.

05. Retention and deletion

We seek to keep personal information only for as long as needed for the purpose for which it is processed, including handling inquiries, maintaining service security, and meeting legal obligations. Technical records and content-provider records may be subject to their respective operators' retention practices.

You may request access to or deletion of information you have sent to the developer, subject to applicable legal exceptions. A request should identify the information concerned so it can be located and assessed. Deleting the app from your device does not automatically delete information held independently by third-party services.

06. Security

Reasonable technical and organizational safeguards are appropriate to the nature of the information and services involved. No internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Avoid including sensitive financial information, passwords, or identity documents in general support messages.

07. Children's privacy

Betão Scores presents sports information. The service is not intended to solicit personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent personal information to the developer, please contact us so that the request can be reviewed and appropriate action taken. Age-related requirements depend on the app's actual features and the laws that apply.

08. Your privacy rights

Depending on your location and the applicable law, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction, or portability of personal information, or to object to certain processing. You may also have a right to withdraw consent where processing relies on consent, and to lodge a complaint with an appropriate supervisory authority.

To make a privacy request, use the Contact page and identify the nature of your request. The contact form submits inquiries through Formspree once the developer has activated the form service.

09. International data processing

Sports information and web infrastructure can be delivered through providers operating in different countries. This can involve processing technical request information outside your country of residence. Where cross-border transfers of personal data require specific safeguards, the responsible operator should apply safeguards appropriate to the applicable legal framework.

10. Changes to this policy

This policy may be revised to reflect changes in Betão Scores, website integrations, applicable law, or actual data practices. Updates will be published on this page with a revised date. Material changes should be communicated through appropriate channels where required by law.
