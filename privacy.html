01. Who this policy covers

This Privacy Policy applies to the Bettao Scores mobile application and the related website at betaoscores.com (together, the “Services”). The developer identified for these Services is OLEKSANDR PLACHYNDA (referred to as “we”, “us” or “our” in this policy).

This website provides sports information and a contact form. Sports results on the website are displayed using a third-party embedded service.

02. Information you may provide or generate

Support messages: If you use our contact form, you may submit your name, email address, selected topic, message and optional device or app-version details. Please avoid including passwords, financial information or other sensitive data.

Website technical information: When you access the website, the hosting service and embedded providers may receive technical details needed to deliver content, such as an IP address, browser information and request timestamps.

App information: Data processed within the Bettao Scores iOS app depends on the features, permissions and third-party services included in the installed version. The developer must ensure the published App Store privacy disclosures and this policy accurately describe the app's actual behavior. This policy does not assert that a specific analytics, advertising, location or account service is present when that has not been established.

03. Why information is used

Information submitted through the contact form is used to read and respond to inquiries, troubleshoot reported problems, handle feedback and process privacy requests. Technical request information may be processed to deliver the website, maintain reliability and protect against abuse.

Information handled by the iOS app should be used only for the functions and purposes actually implemented in the released app, subject to applicable notices and permissions.

04. External services and sharing

Formspree: The contact form uses Formspree to receive and forward submissions. The information you submit is transmitted to Formspree for this purpose. See Formspree's privacy information.

Realtime Sports API: The homepage and contact page embed scoreboards hosted by Realtime Sports API. Loading these embeds connects your browser to that provider, which may receive standard technical request information. See the score provider's website for provider information.

Hosting: The website may be delivered through GitHub Pages and associated infrastructure. Hosting services process technical requests to make the site available.

We do not claim that these are necessarily all services integrated into the iOS application. The app's actual third-party integrations must be checked separately.

05. Storage and retention

Support messages may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to respond, follow up, keep appropriate records or meet legal obligations. Form submissions may also be stored by the form provider under its service settings and policies. Retention periods for any data handled by the iOS app depend on its actual features and configured services.

06. Your rights and choices

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to request access, correction or deletion of personal information, or to object to or restrict certain processing. You may contact us using the support form and select “Privacy request”. We may need to verify a request before taking action.

Device-level settings may allow you to control permissions granted to the app. The availability of a specific control depends on the installed app version and your operating system.

07. Children

If a parent or guardian believes a child has provided personal information through our website, they may contact us through the support form to request review and appropriate action. The age suitability of the mobile app should follow its actual App Store classification and functionality.

08. Security and international processing

We aim to use reasonable safeguards appropriate to the information processed. No online transmission or storage method can be guaranteed completely secure. External providers may process information in jurisdictions different from your own, subject to their policies and applicable legal protections.

09. Changes to this policy

We may revise this policy when our Services, providers or legal obligations change. The current version will be posted on this page with an updated date. Material changes should be reflected in the app's disclosures where required.
