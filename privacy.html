Privacy policy.

How Bettao Scores, Jogo do Tigrinho, and this website handle information, which third-party services may be involved, and how to contact the developer about privacy.

01. Who this policy covers

This Privacy Policy applies to the Bettao Scores mobile application, the Jogo do Tigrinho mobile application, and the related website at betaoscores.com (collectively, the “Services”). Both applications are published or intended to be published by OLEKSANDR PLACHYNDA (referred to as “we”, “us” or “our”).

Bettao Scores is a sports-information application. Jogo do Tigrinho is a separate application. Their features and data practices may differ. Website features, including the sports scoreboards and contact form, do not automatically apply to either mobile app.

02. Information you may provide or generate

Support messages: If you use the website contact form, you may provide your name, email address, selected topic, message, and optional device or app-version details. Please avoid including passwords, payment details, or other sensitive information.

Website technical information: When you access betaoscores.com, its hosting and embedded-content providers may receive technical request information, such as IP address, browser details, and request timestamps.

Bettao Scores application: Data collected or processed by this app depends on the features, permissions, and third-party services included in its released version.

Jogo do Tigrinho application: Data collected or processed by this app likewise depends on its actual released features, permissions, and third-party services. This policy does not assume the app has accounts, advertising, analytics, in-app purchases, payment processing, location access, or real-money gambling functionality. If any such functionality exists, its specific data practices and applicable notices must be disclosed accurately before publication.

The developer should ensure this policy and each app's App Store privacy disclosures reflect what that particular app actually does.

03. Why information is used

Contact-form information is used to respond to questions, investigate reported issues, receive feedback, and handle privacy requests. Technical website information may be processed to deliver content, maintain reliability, and protect against misuse.

For each mobile application, personal information should be processed only for the functions and purposes actually implemented and disclosed for that app, subject to applicable notices and permissions.

04. External services and sharing

Formspree (website): The website contact form uses Formspree to receive and forward messages. Information you submit through the form is transmitted to Formspree. See Formspree's privacy information.

Realtime Sports API (website): The website's homepage and contact page embed live-score widgets from Realtime Sports API. Loading them may send standard technical request information to that provider. See the score provider's website.

GitHub Pages (website hosting): The website may be hosted through GitHub Pages and related infrastructure, which processes technical requests to deliver the site.

Mobile apps: Bettao Scores and Jogo do Tigrinho may use different third-party SDKs or services. The website providers listed above should not be assumed to be integrated into either mobile app. Any app-specific advertising, analytics, payments, or other third-party processing must be described when confirmed.

05. Storage and retention

Support messages may be kept as long as reasonably necessary to respond, follow up, maintain appropriate records, or comply with legal obligations. Formspree may also retain submissions according to its settings and policies.

Any personal information processed by Bettao Scores or Jogo do Tigrinho should be retained only as needed for the disclosed purposes and applicable legal requirements. Exact periods depend on the actual services and features used by each app.

06. Your rights and choices

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to request access, correction, or deletion of personal information, or to object to or restrict certain processing. To make a request about either app or the website, use our support form, select “Privacy request”, and identify whether your request concerns Bettao Scores, Jogo do Tigrinho, or betaoscores.com. We may need to verify your request before taking action.

You can also manage app permissions through your device settings where the relevant controls are available.

07. Children

If a parent or guardian believes a child has provided personal information through our website or either mobile app, they can contact us through the support form for review and appropriate action.

The intended audience, age rating, and any age restrictions for Bettao Scores and Jogo do Tigrinho must reflect each application's actual content and features. This policy does not establish that either app is designed for children.

08. Security and international processing

We aim to use reasonable safeguards appropriate to the information processed. No method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure. Website and app service providers may process information in countries other than your own, subject to their policies and applicable legal protections.

09. Changes to this policy

We may revise this policy when the website, Bettao Scores, Jogo do Tigrinho, third-party providers, or legal requirements change. The latest version will be posted here with its updated date. Material changes will be reflected in the relevant app disclosures where required.
