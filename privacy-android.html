01Overview

Bettao Scores is a sports information application published on Google Play by Hlopkys (referred to as “the developer,” “we,” or “us”). This policy applies to the Android application. The website at betaoscores.com has its own information-handling practices and services, which may differ from those in the Android app.

The iOS version has a separate privacy policy and developer contact.

02Information involved in the service

Accessing online sports information involves network requests. Those requests may include technical connection information, such as an IP address, request time, and device or application details needed to deliver content. The infrastructure or sports-data providers that receive requests may process this information.

If you email the developer, we receive the email address and any information you choose to include in your message.

The app does not require an account and the developer does not ask users to provide their name, email address, or other personal details to use it. The app uses Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) to deliver push notifications. FCM automatically processes technical information, including app-instance registration tokens and related device and app information, for notification delivery. This processing occurs through Google’s Firebase service even though the developer does not directly request personal information from users.

03How information is used

Technical information may be processed to deliver sports content and push notifications, maintain service reliability, and protect against misuse. FCM registration tokens are used to address notifications to app installations. Information sent in support or privacy emails is used to respond to your inquiry and manage related requests.

Where required by applicable law, processing must have an appropriate legal basis, which may include providing a requested service, legitimate interests, legal obligations, or consent.

04Third-party services

The Android app uses Google Firebase Cloud Messaging for push notifications. Google may process FCM registration tokens and other technical data needed to operate the service. See Firebase Privacy and Security for more information. Sports data, app distribution, hosting, and related infrastructure may also be supplied by external providers, which may process technical information when their services are used.

The developer states that the Android app does not use Firebase Analytics, Firebase Crashlytics, or advertising SDKs. The website may use different third-party services; those website integrations should not be assumed to be present in the Android app.

05Retention and deletion

Information received directly by the developer, such as support emails, is kept only as long as reasonably needed to respond, manage follow-up matters, or meet applicable obligations. FCM-related technical information is retained according to Google’s service practices; the developer does not control all of Google’s retention periods. External providers may have separate retention practices.

You can request access to or deletion of personal information held by the developer by emailing zaichka.bosonwz@gmail.com. Please describe the information concerned so we can assess the request. Uninstalling the app does not necessarily delete records held by independent third-party providers.

06Security

We aim to use safeguards appropriate to the information processed. No internet-based system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Please do not send passwords, financial credentials, or identity documents in ordinary support emails.

07Children’s privacy

Bettao Scores is a sports information service and is not intended to solicit personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information directly to the developer, contact us so the matter can be reviewed. Additional protections may apply depending on the audience, actual app functionality, and relevant law.

08Your privacy rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction, or portability of personal information, to object to certain processing, or to withdraw consent where applicable. You may also have the right to contact a data protection authority.

For requests concerning information held by Hlopkys in relation to the Android app, email zaichka.bosonwz@gmail.com.

09International processing

Online sports information and supporting infrastructure may be provided from different countries. Where personal data is transferred across borders, the parties responsible for the processing must apply safeguards required by applicable law.

10Changes to this policy

We may update this policy to reflect changes in the Android application, third-party integrations, applicable requirements, or data practices. The revised version will appear on this page with an updated date. Where required, material changes will be communicated through appropriate channels.
