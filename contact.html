HELPFUL INFORMATION

Before you
reach out.

A few quick answers to common questions about support and live score data.

How do I report an issue with the app?

Choose “Technical support” in the form and describe what happened. Including your device model, iOS version, app version and the steps that led to the issue helps us understand it.

Why might a live score appear delayed?

Live scores on this website are provided by a third-party sports data service and may update with a delay. Refresh intervals and available competitions depend on the provider.

How do I request information about my data?

Select “Privacy request” in the contact form. Please don't send sensitive identification documents unless specifically requested through a secure process.

Will clicking Send Message open my email app?

No. The form sends directly to our form-processing provider, Formspree, and displays a confirmation when your submission is accepted.
