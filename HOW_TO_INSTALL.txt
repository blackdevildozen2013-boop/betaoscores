  contact.html

IMPORTANT: Direct message delivery requires a form service. GitHub Pages cannot deliver messages by itself.

1. Create a free Formspree account at https://formspree.io/
2. Create a new form and set the recipient to your support email.
3. Verify your receiving email and activate the form as instructed by Formspree.
4. Copy the form endpoint, which looks like https://formspree.io/f/abcdefgh
5. Open main.js and find:
   const FORM_ENDPOINT = 'https://formspree.io/f/REPLACE_WITH_YOUR_FORM_ID';
   Replace the entire quoted URL with your actual Formspree endpoint.
6. Upload contact.html, main.js and privacy.html to your repository root, overwriting the existing files.
7. Wait for GitHub Pages to publish, then submit a test message. Check the Formspree dashboard and your receiving inbox.

The button reads SEND MESSAGE. No email application opens. The form shows a success message ONLY after the delivery provider confirms HTTP success; otherwise an error is shown. Until you configure the endpoint, the form clearly reports that delivery is not configured.

Why privacy.html changes: the form now transfers name, email, topic and message to Formspree, a third-party provider. Please review the final policy against your actual practices and Formspree settings.

Free plan limits and availability are subject to Formspree's current terms.
