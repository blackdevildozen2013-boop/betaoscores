Questions answered

Before the first whistle.

Can I subscribe and pay today?

Not yet. This is a concept and early-access interest page. No payment is taken and no premium account is activated.

Are the displayed statistics and predictions real?

No. The interactive dashboard and promotional artwork use explicitly illustrative sample data. Production analytics will require a licensed data source, a server-side processing system and validation of any predictive models.

Will Bettao Scores still have free live scores?

Yes. The existing live-score widgets remain on the main Bettao Scores website. This proposed premium product is a separate analytics experience.

When will Intelligence launch?

No launch date has been announced. The scope and timing depend on data licensing, technical development and testing.
